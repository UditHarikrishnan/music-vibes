Smooth Jazz

Smooth jazz blends traditional jazz with contemporary R&B, creating a relaxed and sophisticated sound. It features mellow saxophone lines, gentle keyboard tones, and subtle rhythms. Unlike more energetic jazz styles, smooth jazz is designed to be calming and widely accessible, making it popular among casual listeners.

Bob James: Born in Missouri, Bob James is widely known as the king of smooth jazz. With 58 albums and a major influence on the genre, he helped introduce millions to jazz through his accessible and soulful style. He is also known for the theme song of the TV show *Taxi*.

George Benson: A legendary guitarist and vocalist, best known for his album *Breezin'*. Benson helped bring smooth jazz into the mainstream, bridging the gap between traditional jazz and modern pop.
