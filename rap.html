What is rap?

Rap is all about rhythm, flow and words. Rappers use their voice like an instrument, matching their lyrics to the beat. It can be confident, emotional, aggressive or chill depending on the artist.

Key parts of rap

  • Flow: how the words sit on the beat
  • Lyrics: storytelling and wordplay
  • Beats: drums, bass and samples
  • Energy: the attitude and delivery

Why people like rap

Rap lets artists talk about real life, struggles, confidence and success. People like it because it feels honest and has energy you can feel in the beat.
