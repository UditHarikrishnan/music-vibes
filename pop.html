Pop Music

Discovering the sound of today.

Introduction

Pop music is one of the world's most dynamic and influential genres, known for its catchy melodies. From classic hits to today's viral pop stars, pop reflects the sounds of each generation, and brings generations together through rhythm, emotions, and creativity.

The Evolution of Pop Music

Pop music has transformed dramatically over the decades, reflecting cultural changes. While the term "pop" originally meant "popular music", it has become a powerful genre with its own identity.

1950s

Pop took shape with artists like Elvis Presley, blending Rock & Roll with rhythm, creating catchy melodies and youthful energy.

1960s

The Beatles took pop worldwide. Pop became trend-setting and deeply connected to youth culture.

1970s

The 70s brought new flavours:
• Disco – ABBA
• Pop Rock – David Bowie
Pop became more dance-oriented and visually expressive.

1980s

The MTV era changed everything. Michael Jackson, Madonna, Prince and Whitney Houston used visuals, choreography and bold production to define pop.

1990s

Pop blended with R&B and dance. Teen culture exploded with Britney Spears and the Spice Girls.

2000s

Pop became experimental, blending hip-hop, electronic, rock and R&B. Beyoncé, Rihanna and Lady Gaga shaped a new era.

2010s

Streaming rose massively. Artists like Bruno Mars, Ariana Grande and Taylor Swift became global giants.

2020s

Pop is now shaped by social media trends, especially TikTok. Artists like Charli XCX and Chappell Roan thrive through viral culture.

Pop Music Today

Pop continues to evolve but keeps its core: catchy melodies, emotional connection, and global appeal. Its ability to adapt is what keeps it one of the most influential genres.
