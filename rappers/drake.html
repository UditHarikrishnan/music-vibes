Drake

Canadian rapper, singer and songwriter known for mixing rap and R&B.

About Drake

Drake is one of the biggest names in modern rap. His music blends rap, R&B and pop, and he is known for switching between singing and rapping on the same track. Over the years he has released albums that broke streaming records and stayed at the top of the charts.

A lot of his songs talk about fame, relationships, success and his life coming up from Toronto. His style helped make melodic rap more popular worldwide.

Quick facts

  • Started out acting in Degrassi
  • Breakthrough mixtape: So Far Gone
