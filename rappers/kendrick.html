Kendrick Lamar

One of the most respected lyricists in rap, known for storytelling and powerful messages.

About Kendrick

Kendrick Lamar is known for his deep storytelling, complex lyrics and socially conscious themes. His albums often explore identity, race, politics and personal struggles, making him one of the most influential rappers of his generation.

Albums like good kid, m.A.A.d city, To Pimp a Butterfly and DAMN. have received critical acclaim, with Kendrick winning multiple Grammy Awards and even a Pulitzer Prize for music.

Quick facts
