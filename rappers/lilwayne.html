Lil Wayne

Rap icon known for punchlines, mixtapes and influencing a whole new generation of artists.

About Lil Wayne

Lil Wayne is considered one of the most influential rappers of all time. His clever punchlines, raspy voice and nonstop mixtape grind helped shape the modern sound of rap.

Wayne's mixtape series Dedication and albums like Tha Carter III made him a global star. He also played a huge role in launching the careers of Drake and Nicki Minaj through Young Money Entertainment.

Quick facts

  • From New Orleans, Louisiana
  • Joined Cash Money Records at age 9
  • Founder of Young Money Entertainment
  • Known for punchline-heavy flow
