About Travis Scott

Travis Scott is one of the biggest artists in modern trap music. His sound is built around heavy bass, dark atmospheric production and strong use of Auto-Tune to create a dreamy, spacey feel.

His album ASTROWORLD was a huge success, praised for its production, creativity and visuals. Travis is also known for his high-energy performances and unique stage designs.

Quick facts

  • From Houston, Texas
  • Founder of Cactus Jack Records
  • Breakthrough hit: Antidote
  • Known for ASTROWORLD Festival
