Nicki Minaj

Rapper known for sharp verses, big personality and switching voices and flows.

About Nicki

Nicki Minaj is one of the most successful female rappers ever. She is known for her fast flows, clever punchlines and the way she can switch between different voices and characters in her verses.

She has released hit albums and has been featured on loads of songs with other artists. Her style mixes rap, pop and R&B, and she has a huge fanbase around the world.

Quick facts

  • From Trinidad & Tobago, raised in New York
  • Signed to Young Money with Lil Wayne and Drake
  • Known for colourful visuals and strong image
  • Big influence for many newer female rappers
