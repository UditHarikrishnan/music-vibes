Frank Ocean

Introspective lyrics and experimental sounds that push the boundaries of R&B.

Frank Ocean is an American singer and songwriter. His accolades include two Grammy Awards and a Brit Award. He is often credited as a pioneer of alternative R&B. Both of his studio albums were listed on Rolling Stone's "500 Greatest Albums of All Time". He was born on October 28, 1987 in Long Beach, California.

Albums: Channel Orange, Blonde and Nostalgia, Ultra. These are his main projects so far.
