H.E.R.

Information

Strong lyrics and emotional vocals.

Gabriella Sarmiento Wilson, known professionally as H.E.R., is an American R&B singer-songwriter. She has won an Academy Award, a Children's & Family Emmy Award, and five Grammy Awards. She has also been nominated for a Golden Globe Award, three American Music Awards, and four Billboard Music Awards. Born June 27, 1997 in Vallejo, California.

Albums: Back of My Mind, H.E.R. Volume 1, and several follow-up projects.
