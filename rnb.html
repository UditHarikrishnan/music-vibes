What is R&B?

Rhythm and blues, commonly known as R&B, is a musical genre developed by Black Americans in the 1940s that’s been continuously refined through the present day. R&B derived from gospel, jazz, folk, and traditional blues music and emerged in tandem with rock ‘n’ roll.

Contemporary R&B is often driven by keyboards, synthesizers, strong bass lines, and looped drum beats. It has a lot in common with hip hop. Modern R&B songs regularly top charts, making it one of the most commercially successful genres in today’s music industry.
